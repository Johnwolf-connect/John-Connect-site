# Approved cinematic transition and flock choreography — 2026-11-04

## Placement
Between the homepage and Design Tools section, directly before the approved portfolio logo gallery.

## Transition
- Scroll-controlled vertical curved wipe, sweeping upward from bottom (not diagonal), revealing the white Design Tools section.
- Homepage subtly recedes behind the wipe. Reverse naturally when scrolling upward.
- Preserve black, white, and purple site styling.

## Design Tools choreography — updated order
1. Curved page transition reveals the section, with the heading initially hidden.
2. Thirteen actual software brand logos enter from the left as a coordinated flock, following related curved flight paths with small individual delays and banking.
3. Logos fan out into straight horizontal rows with horizontally offset columns: 3 top, 7 center, 3 bottom. Use slight overshoot, buoyant settling, and gentle idle float.
4. **Only after the flock has landed**, pause briefly, then reveal the Design Tools heading with a subtle fade and upward movement. Keep heading high on the page.
5. Hold the full arrangement long enough for appreciation and interaction. Nearby logos dodge the cursor with coordinated ripple-like response and return to their slots.
6. On onward scroll, the logos flee smoothly to the right in coordinated motion, revealing the existing approved gallery.

Now I can’t speak for

everyone... But at least for me,

Design is life!

So… what is the human nature of design to me?

Well, it's the creation of the mind's eye, a way to take something intangible like dreams, thoughts, imaginations, ideas, and turn them into things that can literally function in the physical world.

The first design I ever saved was on one of these. What is it?

Well… if you know, you know. I was fortunate enough to start early, knowing what makes designs unique and how to communicate that is a practice that can be explained but is better experienced.

Some people say “art is subjective.” However, I believe there is always a fine line.

It twists, turns, it bends, and breaks, but somewhere along those lines, we genuinely agree with appealing visuals that intentional lines create.

What am I good at? Let's see.

Photoshop, Illustrator, Procreate, graphic design, copywriting, visual design, brand identity, logo development, presentations, and recently website development, machine learning, and AI prompt writing. Just to keep it short.
